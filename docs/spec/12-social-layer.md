# 12 · Social layer — share, discuss, improve

> **Intent.** Every resource can be **shared**, **discussed**, and **improved**
> with other users through three orthogonal primitives — Grant, Discussion,
> Proposal — each defined against a generic entity reference, never a specific
> kind, and composing with any of the six social nouns (the relation graph's
> five plus Discussion itself). The full design + phased backlog:
> [`docs/design/social-layer.md`](../design/social-layer.md). Privacy rules are
> inherited, not invented: existence-hiding walls, REDACTED wire faces, INV-9
> absence classification.

All are now `own`-gated, and the rail gains the generic `GrantsPanel` so a routine can actually be shared (its social surface existed with no door to it). Locked by an `e2e/social-layer.spec.ts` walk that asserts the read-only page AND that landing the visitor's offer changes the routine's real cadence. Surfaced by the generic `ProposalsPanel` on the routine page. **This also finished SOCIAL-4 for routines:** a grant now OPENS a schedule (it had been wired for projects and sessions only, so a shared routine was readable by nobody), with the write routes retightened to OWNERSHIP so the widened read can't hand a visitor the edit/run/delete. **Session fork ✅ (2026-07-22)** — `POST /sessions/:id/fork` continues a conversation from ONE TURN into the caller's namespace, carrying `Session.forkedFrom` provenance and ringing the source's owner (`social.forked`, whose door lands on the OWNER's conversation, never the forker's private copy). **Adjudicated — a fork is NOT a proposal**, though the design doc filed it as one: a proposal is a change offered TO a resource and landed by its owner's consent, and a fork changes nothing about the source, so there is nothing to consent to. It follows `forkAgent`'s shape — a direct act gated on the `propose` tier, credited by a bell. The cut is AT the named turn inclusive (taking the whole thread would make the locus decorative), and the affordance rides the same `LocusGutter` the turn already had, through a generic action slot. Locked by `tests/social.test.ts` + a cross-tenant walk in `e2e/social-layer.spec.ts`.
